# Enzo Fernandes

**Electrical Engineering graduate · Hardware testing · Embedded systems**

I graduated in Electrical Engineering at **UNESP in August 2026**. I work with electronic hardware testing, component-level troubleshooting and laboratory operations at TDM GRID, where my formal role is **Assistente Técnico de Proteção e Controle**.

My work includes bench instrumentation, Python scripts for reports and equipment logs, and technical collaboration with the manufacturer Vebko. In my academic and team projects, I combine power electronics, PCB design and ESP32 firmware.

## Selected projects

### [ESP32 BLDC ESC — my final degree project](https://github.com/enzolfernandes/esp32-bldc-esc)

For my published thesis, I developed and analyzed a BLDC controller prototype. I brought together Altium PCB design, LTspice simulations and C/C++ firmware with PlatformIO, FreeRTOS, a hardware abstraction layer, ADC acquisition and serial/Wi-Fi telemetry.

I carried out static bench tests and investigated faults in the prototype. My repository brings together the hardware files, firmware and technical documentation.

[Published thesis — UNESP](https://hdl.handle.net/11449/327581) · [Project and results](https://github.com/enzolfernandes/esp32-bldc-esc) · [Hardware](https://github.com/enzolfernandes/esp32-bldc-esc/tree/main/Hardware)

### [CombatIO — my contributions with the TERA robotics team](https://github.com/enzolfernandes/combatIO)

I contributed to the [TERA team's project](https://github.com/roboticatera/CombatIO), using ESP32, C++, PlatformIO and a PS4 controller over Bluetooth. My repository is a fork that preserves the team's history.

My contributions include changes to locomotion commands, motor-stop commands on controller disconnection, and adaptation to a unidirectional weapon ESC.

[Locomotion changes](https://github.com/enzolfernandes/combatIO/commit/12527322fff6e27bc620f852db3d01cad864fc83) · [Disconnection handling](https://github.com/enzolfernandes/combatIO/commit/440a736070e4b5e61b1fc55070fb83deb96480b1) · [ESC adaptation](https://github.com/enzolfernandes/combatIO/commit/a3fbd44a5bf5782af3b5fd2ee67f7c70121aa490)
